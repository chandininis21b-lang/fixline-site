How it works

Three steps from problem to fix

  1. 01

    Describe the issue

    Type or talk through what's happening — a frozen app, a login that won't work, a printer that's gone quiet. No ticket forms, no categories to guess at.

  2. 02

    The agent investigates

    It asks clarifying questions, pulls device and account context, and checks known issues before proposing a fix — the same triage a senior technician would run.

  3. 03

    Resolved or routed

    Simple issues are fixed on the spot with guided steps. Anything that needs a human is escalated instantly with the full conversation and diagnostics attached.
